## Fixed phases and source binding

Entry: `scripts/qualification/pr1142/native-enho-entry.ts`. The fixed private root is
`~/.local/state/arc1-qualification/pr1142-native-enhancement-owned-stub-v1`.
All phases use the reviewed full SHA and exact Node22.21.1 runtime; source and package-lock binding,
clean checkout, private evidence modes and the shared runtime restrictions remain mandatory.

- `--prepare-reviewed-enhancement-owned-stub-v1 FULL_SHA` saves a fresh immutable intent locally.
- `--preflight-reviewed-enhancement-owned-stub-v1 FULL_SHA` performs seven fixed GETs: discovery,
  spot, interface metadata/source, two exact typed owner absences and the inactive inventory.
- After preparation and that preflight, capture actual fresh zero-result SM12 suffix and MARIAN
  searches. Preserve unmodified JPEGs with honest `.jpg` names and the sample/source/intent-bound
  baseline receipt. An operator must inspect displayed filters/results; a hash cannot prove pixels
  depict the claimed user or freshness.
- `--check-reviewed-enhancement-owned-stub-v1 FULL_SHA SAMPLE_ID` is offline, read-only and
  nonclaiming; it does not import the runtime, load credentials or contact SAP.
- `--execute-reviewed-enhancement-owned-stub-v1 FULL_SHA SAMPLE_ID` independently rereads every
  prerequisite and consumes one permanent attempt before loading credentials. A failed attempt is
  never reset or replayed. Only the operator may run these actual phases after separate review.

The shared client factory supplies the actual fixed pinned mutation policy. This is an explicitly
Basic-native contract experiment; the qualification-local private-adapter context does not claim PP
identity or grant public availability. The original freshness boundary and at most 240 seconds,
384 HTTP attempts and 2 MiB aggregate response bytes apply across all stages. Final dispatch repeats
source/policy/stop/deadline proofs after queue/auth waits and synchronous verification. Owning
UNLOCK/session close retain separate bounded cleanup allowance after stop or expiry.

Lock-related response bodies and unrelated inactive inventory bodies are hash-only. Confirmed
session closure is the production cleanup predicate; no missing raw close response is invented.
After either success or failure, capture fresh post-run SM12 evidence and independently audit the
immutable receipts before reporting native completion. Any retained owner needs a separately reviewed
fixed continuation/cleanup rather than rerunning the old root.
